Every artboard is the live app. Click through any of them, or open the clickable prototype and use the sign-in screen to switch roles.

Demo choir
Ottawa Regional Youth Choir, on its ORYC theme layer. The blue is the design system’s placeholder until ORYC supplies its brand colour and logo; people and venues are fictional.

Layout
Desktop: header and sidebar. Tablet: header and drawer. Phone: header and bottom tabs. Same for every role.
Theme and language
